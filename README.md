# LoL-Breaker — Quantitative League of Legends Modeling & Betting Intelligence

Standalone application and quantitative modeling system for League of Legends match outcome prediction, tournament simulation, and betting analytics.

**LoL-Breaker** integrates pre-match causal match-winner models, tournament distribution simulators, and standalone map prop estimators (`src/`, `conf/`) with a complete decision-time betting intelligence platform (`betting_app/`, `client/`). The application does **not** place bets automatically.

### 5.4 Betting Strategy & Optimizer's Curse Decomposition

<details>
<summary><b>📖 View Strategy Stack Definitions & Econometric Metric Formulas</b></summary>

#### Strategy Stacks & Metric Nomenclature Guide

To evaluate where true profit comes from, five strategy configurations and econometric metrics are benchmarked:

| Strategy Stack Label | Exact Active Pipeline & Rules | Purpose in Ablation |
|---|---|---|
| **Unconstrained `C0`** | Pure `C0` without shrinkage or safety gates; flat $\text{EV} \ge 5\%$ | Baseline: tests whether a raw sports model can beat bookmakers alone. |
| **`Hybrid-50` + Divergence-Gate** | 50/50 Bayesian logit shrinkage + `Market-Divergence-Gate` ($\le 0.85$) | Tests shrinkage and rejection of severe bookmaker probability disagreements. |
| **Optimal Synergy** | `Hybrid-50` + Divergence-Gate + `Model-Consensus-Veto` ($\le 0.10$) + Odds $\le 4.00$ | Production baseline: requires neural attention and linear form agreement. |
| **Optimal Synergy + DRO** | Optimal Synergy + `Wasserstein-Robust-Gate` ($\varepsilon = 0.025$) | Robust production gate: prunes bets vulnerable to probability shifts. |
| **Multi-Year Hybrid (2021–26)** | Optimal Synergy on 6-season OddsPortal dataset ($N=11,609$) | Macro validation: tests long-term strategy survival across 0% and 12% tax. |

*Metric Definitions:*
- **Claimed EV (Promise):** Expected return promised by the model: $(p_{\text{model}} \cdot 0.88 \cdot O) - 1$.
- **True Edge (Net CLV):** Edge against sharp closing market: $(p_{\text{close}} \cdot 0.88 \cdot O) - 1$.
- **Optimizer's Curse Bias ($B = \text{Claimed EV} - \text{Net CLV}$):** Positive selection bias from cherry-picking estimation errors.
- **Match Luck Noise ($\eta = \text{Realized Yield} - \text{Net CLV}$):** Binary coinflip outcome variance that decays as $\sigma / \sqrt{N}$.
- **Realized Yield:** Actual observed return on capital: $(Y \cdot 0.88 \cdot O) - 1$.

</details>

*Mathematical Profit & Noise Decomposition:*

$$
\text{Realized Yield} = \underbrace{\text{Claimed EV}}_{\text{Model Promise}} - \underbrace{B}_{\text{Optimizer's Curse}} + \underbrace{\eta}_{\text{Match Luck Noise}} = \textbf{True Edge (Net CLV)} + \eta
$$

- **Optimizer's Curse Bias ($B = \text{Claimed EV} - \text{Net CLV}$):** Systematic positive selection bias from picking bets where estimation error happened to be high. **Never vanishes with sample size $N$**. On unconstrained C0, it produced **+45.62 pp** of fake edge. Bounded shrinkage ($\alpha=0.50$), `Market-Divergence-Gate`, and `Model-Consensus-Veto` successfully compressed this bias down to **+8.57 pp**.
- **Match Luck Noise ($\eta = \text{Realized Yield} - \text{Net CLV}$):** Zero-mean binomial outcome variance ($\sigma_{\text{bet}} \approx 109.8\%$) that **decays to zero as $\sigma / \sqrt{N}$**. On the 50-bet production ledger, **+19.42 pp** of temporary lucky variance boosted wallet yield to **+23.91%**, while the true long-term compounding edge is **Net CLV = +4.83%**.

### 5.5 Econometric Audit: True Profit ($\text{Net CLV}$) vs. Noise

<details>
<summary><b>📐 View Deep-Dive Econometric Audit: Hypothesis Tests, Variance Ratio (104.5x) & Time-to-Profit (N_min) Schedule</b></summary>

#### A. The Exact Three-Part Bet Decomposition

For any placed bet $i$ with tax multiplier $c = 1 - \tau = 0.88$, decimal odds $O_i$, model probability $\hat{p}_i$, fair closing probability $p_{\text{close}, i}$, and match outcome $Y_i \in \{0, 1\}$:

$$
\text{Realized Yield}_i = \text{Claimed EV}_i - \underbrace{c \cdot O_i (\hat{p}_i - p_{\text{close}, i})}_{B_i \text{ (Optimizer's Curse)}} + \underbrace{c \cdot O_i (Y_i - p_{\text{close}, i})}_{\eta_i \text{ (Match Luck Noise)}}
$$

where Realized Yield $c \cdot O_i Y_i - 1 = +24.26\%$ and Claimed EV $c \cdot O_i \hat{p}_i - 1 = +13.41\%$. Mean Optimizer's Curse bias is $B = +8.57\text{ pp}$ and mean Match Luck Noise is $\eta = +19.42\text{ pp}$.
- **Pure C0 Claimed EV:** `+22.05%`
- **Shrunk Hybrid Claimed EV:** `+13.41%`
- **Optimizer's Curse Removed by Shrinkage:** `8.64 pp` (plus 97 toxic bets vetoed by Guard B & Thesis Veto)
- **True Long-Term Mathematical Profit ($\text{Net CLV}$):** `+4.83%` per bet ($p_{\text{close}}$ expected wins: $25.67 / 50$)
- **Identity Mathematical Residual:** $\lt 10^{-15}$ (Exact equality across all 50 bets)

#### B. Hypothesis Testing & Variance Ratio ($N=50$ Production Bets)

| Metric | Point Estimate | Sample Std ($s$) | Std Error (SE) | $t$-Statistic ($p$-Value) | 95% Bootstrap CI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **Realized Yield** | **+24.26%** | 116.50% | 16.48% | 1.472 ($p=0.1473$) | [-7.55%, +54.83%] |
| **Raw CLV (Price Edge)** | **+19.13%** | 12.95% | 1.83% | 10.446 ($p \lt 10^{-13}$) | [+15.55%, +22.71%] |
| **Net CLV (True Edge)** | **+4.83%** | 11.39% | 1.61% | **3.000 ($p=0.0042$)** | **[+1.68%, +7.98%]** |

$$
\text{Variance Ratio} = \frac{\text{Var}(\text{Realized Yield})}{\text{Var}(\text{Net CLV})} = \frac{1.1650^2}{0.1139^2} = \mathbf{104.5} \times
$$

*Finding:* Realized wallet PnL has $104.5\times$ higher variance than Net CLV. Proving edge via Net CLV requires only 15–30 bets, whereas proving edge via wallet PnL requires $>1,000$ bets.

#### C. Time-to-Profit ($N_{\min}$) & Noise Decay Schedule

At average odds $\bar{O} = 2.53$ under 12% tax, per-bet binomial noise is $\sigma_{\text{bet}} = 109.79\%$. After $N$ bets:

$$
