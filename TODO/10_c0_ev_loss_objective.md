M-price has three coefficients centered(1,1,0), bounds(nonnegative,nonnegative,free); H-price additionally has native coefficient centered0/nonnegative. Four arms: H-price/likelihood, H-price/return_mse, M-price/likelihood, M-price/return_mse. Each fits lambda{.01,.1,1}: exactly12 formal head fits,0 base fits. Both loss objectives use identical TRAIN membership. Both select on identical CAL **two-sided mean return-MSE**, not profit, with tie tolerance1e-12 favoring larger lambda; no CAL refit. This equal CAL rule isolates TRAIN loss; previous LL-selected heads are retained no-fit diagnostics, not controls with unequal tuning.

For per-fixture outcome y and coherent p_A=sigmoid(X theta), p_B=1-p_A, fixed net multiplier t=.88:

`return_mse_i = .5 * ((t*O_A*(p_A-y))^2 + (t*O_B*((1-p_A)-(1-y)))^2) = .5*t^2*(O_A^2+O_B^2)*(p_A-y)^2`.

Optimize mean raw-probability return-MSE plus lambda/2 centered squared L2, or mean stable raw-logit Bernoulli NLL plus the same penalty. No normalization that changes the declared loss/penalty tradeoff. Reject nonfinite derived weights/objectives, booleans, odds<=1, invalid labels/probabilities. Use deterministic center and existing L-BFGS-B options maxiter2000/ftol1e-12/gtol1e-8/maxls50. Return-MSE through sigmoid is **nonconvex**: certify gradient/stationarity and independent local solver agreement where applicable, never claim global optimality. Stop and retain an unconverged candidate/attempt; no second starts or adaptive retries. Raw training output separate from epsilon1e-6 scoring/CAL/EV output; record clipping. Side swap must complement p and preserve both objectives.
