**The next sports-model experiment should test the existing raw specialist with the existing native12 C0 anchor, using the same small learned probability connector—not a larger gate or another architecture sweep.** Add a matched C0-only logit-slope calibration control to test gain beyond a global sharpness adjustment. ## 4. What combination research actually supports

Forecast combination has a long empirical history: Bates and Granger found complementary forecasts could reduce error, estimating weights from past errors.[3] That principle supports the question “does the specialist add information to this anchor?” It does not select a particular LoL mixture or prove that an optimized combination dominates its components.

Stacking requires meta-training predictions for observations excluded from the corresponding base-model training batch.[4] Here that must be strengthened to the project’s release-aware chronological contract. Random cross-validation of the whole time series would not be a valid substitute, and base-weight OOF does not undo architecture selection on the same years.

The ECB combination study is especially relevant to restraint. It reports that estimated optimal weights can have substantial estimation error when forecast count is large relative to the time-series sample, and sophisticated combinations did not robustly dominate the simple average across its tasks.[5] These are macroeconomic results, not transferable LoL gains, but they identify a real estimation-variance tradeoff. Thousands of related matches and nine 2026 calendar blocks do not provide thousands of independent temporal regimes.

A mixture-of-experts gate is useful when different experts handle meaningful subsets of the task.[7] We do not yet have evidence that a particular **pre-origin observable** reliably predicts which of these LoL experts should dominate. Historical league residuals, observed outcomes or a few memorable failures are not a valid gate design. The two larger local correction heads already failed their respective two-comparator gates.

Gneiting and Ranjan show that coherence, calibration and dispersion need separate treatment when pooling predictive distributions.[6] Their density and discrete-outcome results have stated conditions; it would be wrong to infer that every binary convex pool must worsen empirical calibration. The defensible lesson is narrower: **pooling calibrated components is not an automatic calibration-preserving operation**. We must assess the resulting probability.

Identity-centered residual adapters are successful in the cited visual-domain work.[8] They offer a design analogy—retain a useful anchor and regularize modifications—not evidence that inserting adapters into A0 will beat the current one-parameter connector. A0 already contains a rating offset, history attention, native scoreline objective and auxiliary performance loss. “Add a residual” is not a new mechanism by itself.

**Decision:** retain simple, symmetry-preserving combination as the next controlled question. Add capacity only after a stable, cutoff-available conditional residual has been identified and evaluated against a simpler control. Do not reinterpret this rule as requiring untouched future data before any historical hypothesis development.

## 5. Why lower global LL can coexist with overstated EV

### 5.1 The relevant conditioning information

Log loss and Brier remain appropriate primary probability scores. Strict propriety rewards the true distribution in the expected scoring problem.[1] If a forecast were the true conditional probability given **all information used by a selection policy**, its conditional mean error would vanish for selections measurable from that information. The problem is not that proper scores are inherently incompatible with betting.

The issue is that the sports model and the price-based decision may use different information. Even exact probability reliability,

\[
E[Y\mid p_s]=p_s,
\]

does not imply

\[
E[Y\mid p_s,q_{market},\text{selection}]=p_s.
\]

The conditioning distinction is consistent with the forecast-calibration framework of Gneiting and Resin.[2] It is stronger than merely observing that small ECE has sampling noise: a perfectly reliable probability predictor can still fail after conditioning on additional informative prices. The finite counterexample below proves the logical possibility directly.

For a one-unit stake, decimal price \(o\), tax fraction \(t\), selected-side probability \(p\) and binary outcome \(y\), predicted-minus-realized net return is

\[
e=(1-t)o(p-y).
\]

A small probability error is magnified by the effective payout. Selecting large positive model-versus-price edges changes the evaluated population. This does **not** prove the project’s errors are caused by a particular omitted roster, patch, region or market factor; it explains why global calibration summaries cannot certify the selected policy.

### 5.2 New exact decomposition of the frozen selection

The existing market scenario has 1,619 fixtures: Pinnacle T−1h, strict-prior-day sports features, the old diagnostic’s 24-hour quote-freshness rule and actual-roster scenario. It is **not** the future Plan06 300-second admission contract, the application’s selection policy or the old 387 prior-context sample.

Keep the original A0-selected IDs, sides and prices fixed. There are 328 selections at raw net EV >5%, including 74 whose **original A0** EV was in (5%,10%]. These 74 are not necessarily the connector’s own near-threshold band. Let \(m=0.88o\) and let \(q\) be the existing normalized two-sided inverse-odds probability. Row by row:

\[
m(p-y)=\underbrace{m(p-q)}_{\text{model–market gap}}+\underbrace{m(q-y)}_{\text{market residual}}.
\]

|---|---:|---:|---:|

### 5.4 Exact counterexample: calibrated sports probabilities, false +7.5% EV

Consider four equally likely contexts. The sports forecast of team A is 0.4,0.4,0.6,0.6. True probabilities are respectively:

\[
352/1075,\quad508/1075,\quad567/1075,\quad723/1075.
\]

Within each sports-probability group, the mean true probability equals the forecast exactly. This is an informative, perfectly reliable two-level predictor—not a constant coin-flip forecast. Set fair two-sided odds to \(1/q\) and \(1/(1-q)\), apply 12% tax, allow at most one side and select only predicted net EV >5%.

Only contexts one and four qualify. Each displays **+7.5% predicted EV**, has **−12% true expected net return**, and therefore overstates value by **19.5pp**. The other two contexts have maximum predicted EV of only \(1/945\), below the threshold. Exact rational arithmetic verifies every equality; no simulated outcome or fitted model is involved.

This proves possibility, not the mechanism or magnitude of LoL error. It also does not contradict decision-calibration theory. Zhao et al. calibrate relative to specified downstream decision makers,[9] while Hu and Wu define worst-case calibration decision loss for payoff-bounded tasks.[11] Their information, task and payoff conditions must remain intact. A variable-price policy with additional observed context is not licensed by a calibration statement conditioning only on the sports probability.

### 5.5 What to change—and what not to infer

**[INFERENCE]** For betting, the probability eventually used for value estimates should be tested as a separately identified market-informed forecast, with a market-only calibration control and a modest nonlinear market-only control. Otherwise, a gain can come entirely from learning the bookmaker’s probability conversion or margin structure rather than new sports information. Egidi et al. provide a primary sports example of combining historical information with bookmaker-derived information, but their football result does not validate a LoL head.[12]

Multicalibration addresses a specified class of subpopulations, not every future adaptively discovered betting subset.[10] It does not justify a large league×odds×confidence correction grid on a sparse archive. Walsh and Joshi’s NBA study motivates evaluating calibration rather than classification accuracy alone, but its one-season betting experiment, chosen strategies and bankroll-based ROI definition are not evidence that minimizing ECE beats LL/Brier or certifies this project’s turnover-based returns.[13] Finite-sample calibration diagnostics require care even before policy conditioning.[18]

Do **not** flatten every probability, delete high-confidence upsets, fit on selected losses, optimize in-sample ECE, or change the sports-only identity to use odds covertly. The project’s earlier controls do not support those interventions.

## 6. Sports representation literature does not reveal a missing ready-made feature

The sports track initially favored performance-informed player state. Checking it against the repository’s actual novelty record changed that recommendation.

TrueSkill2 provides a sound chronology example: predict a match using team/squad composition, then update skills using match-end information. Its reported gains are from Halo5, not professional LoL.[19] PandaSkill models LoL end-game performance separately by role and feeds resulting scores into OpenSkill FFA updates; its meta-rating addresses fragmented regional pools.[20] These are relevant mechanisms, but the project already tested a close PScore→FFA construction: 2024+ LL 0.6171 versus player Glicko 0.6163. A separate bounded zero-sum box-score adjustment was rejected after worsening LL/AUC/ECE. Neither should reappear under a new label as an untested idea.

SIDO models contextual own/ally/enemy performance, but explicitly reports substantial uncertainty in teammate/enemy effects.[21] Causality-inspired adjusted plus-minus addresses lineup confounding in soccer and basketball under strong assumptions.[23] Both make teammate attribution worth understanding; neither demonstrates an incremental pre-match A0 gain. Professional rosters often co-occur, so separating individual and teammate effects requires more than a flexible model.

Dynamic Bradley–Terry provides precedent for changing ability,[22] but A0 already has token age, inactivity, recent-map attention, co-play and rating updates. The project’s H365 target weighting failed. Literature support for dynamics is not evidence for repeating that decay recipe.

The champion-experience study reports prediction after champions have been selected in ranked games.[24] That information is unavailable for an ordinary pre-draft professional forecast. Historical champion tendencies could be a different hypothesis, but this paper does not establish their incremental signal, and no such experiment is authorized here.

**Not recommended now:** another role/performance/RD feature or score-to-rating update. A genuinely distinct joint player-state model retaining off-diagonal teammate covariance remains a possible research question, but neither its novelty relative to the complete native system nor its identifiability from available roster turnover has been established. It is not promoted into the next experiment.

The already positive standalone FamilyMapBridge result also remains separate from its failed A0 integration. A useful component is not automatically a useful addition to a stronger assembled model.

## 7. Proposed bounded sports experiment

**Status: proposed, not run.** This is one new hypothesis with a matched nuisance control, not a menu to sweep until something passes.

### Hypothesis and arms

The existing raw trio contains useful information beyond native12 C0; a regularized one-weight connector can use it without changing C0’s base training, calibration or scoreline semantics.

- **C0:** unchanged native12 assembled series probability.
- **C0-cal:** one nonnegative logit slope, \(\sigma(s\operatorname{logit}(p_{C0}))\), shrunk toward identity \(s=1\). This is a matched simple calibration control, not an additional specialist.
- **Candidate:** \((1-\alpha)p_{C0}+\alpha p_{raw}\), with \(0\leq\alpha\leq1\), shrunk toward \(\alpha=0\).
- **External fixed comparators:** retained connector03 and canonical annual A0. Preserve the earlier fixed75/25 diagnostic and rejected native6+12 result as context, not as outcome-selected new candidates.

**Control boundary:** this tests improvement beyond global slope-only recalibration, not beyond every possible C0-only calibration function and not a causal proof of a new sports factor. Both arms preserve A/B complementarity. A free logit intercept is deliberately excluded: at p=0.5 it maps both orientations to \(\sigma(b)\), so symmetry requires b=0. A future richer calibration-only comparator would need an explicitly symmetric function and a separately frozen scope; it is not added after seeing this experiment's results.

Both learned arms use mean Bernoulli LL plus the same identity-centered quadratic penalty. Freeze the existing four penalties **0.0001,0.001,0.01,0.1**, the existing nine rolling origins from July2024 through July2026, the 500-row minimum and past-validation selection rule. Ties choose stronger shrinkage. No BO-specific coefficient, intercept, league name, price, new expert or enlarged grid.

This adds **36 scalar connector fits plus 36 scalar calibration-control fits; zero base-model fits**. It is intentionally not claimed to be an unchanged rerun of run03: the anchor changes and a matched C0 calibration control is added. No coefficient has been estimated in this research report.

### Chronology and population

1. Pin genuine C0 and raw-trio probabilities, source/checkpoint identities, release dates and side orientation before fitting. Reuse existing probabilities only where their full prior-origin contract passes.
2. Fit and select using released labels strictly before each head origin. The 2024–25 raw design remains exposed closed head development, never independent confirmation.
3. Primary assessment remains all **3,103 canonical 2026 targets through September8**, with the same January/April/July head origins. This is development only. No convenient market-only intersection.
4. Retain all 11,550 rows for coverage. Earlier delivered candidate rows would be unchanged **C0 warmup**, explicitly distinguished from connector03’s A0 warmup. A full-cohort improvement caused by that anchor substitution is not evidence of active-head improvement on those earlier years.
5. The proposal is full-context series-winner inference. Do not invent unsupported no-W20/no-organization connector modes; canonical optional-context modes remain available and unchanged. Do not pass the pooled series probability through a BO projection again.

### Gates and stopping rule

Before execution, freeze exact memberships, artifact hashes, code, origins, probabilities and the following **joint development gate**:

- Candidate-minus-C0, candidate-minus-C0-cal and candidate-minus-connector03 monthly paired LL intervals must each have upper endpoint below zero, with Brier point estimates no worse than each corresponding comparator.
- Use at least 5,000 paired monthly draws, report match-weighted and equal-month effects, all nine months and source-name/year sensitivity. A favorable proxy result cannot substitute for the primary estimand or become an edition-level claim.
- Retain the existing +0.005 LL noninferiority guard framework for BO3, BO5, major and source-unseen-player groups, now checked against C0 and canonical A0. Missing support is unresolved, not an automatic pass.
- Report fixed-favorite calibration cells, ECE with support, confidence errors, tails, chronology, side symmetry, cold/empty history behavior, exact coverage and nonmutation. No standalone ECE or tail-count success criterion.
- Preserve fixed selections and own-policy selections as separate optional diagnostics on the same archived market scenario. They cannot clear G0 or replace genuine decision-time evidence.
- If a required contrast or guard fails, record **NOT_ADOPTED** for this experiment and stop. Do not increase epochs, change penalties, add a gate or remove leagues using its outcome. A later hypothesis needs a separate rationale and scope.

These intervals remain nominal, not a correction for the entire adaptive research history. Even passing all development gates retains a candidate; it does not authorize production or establish superiority to every historical model.

**[INFERENCE]** This is preferable to a new network because it tests the anchor/specialist increment at low model complexity. It could fail because the derivative reflects exposed-sample noise or the learned weight is unstable. A pass demonstrates improvement beyond these specified controls only; residual nonlinear calibration explanations remain possible, even if the global slope control is beaten.

## 9. Evaluation: continue development without manufacturing confirmation

### Two distinct lanes

**Historical development:** bounded experiments on exposed years, complete search lineage, fixed controls, negative results retained, no “untouched test” claims. The proposed C0 experiment belongs here.

**Genuine confirmation:** freeze the complete recipe, data-admission policy and future-valid artifact before collecting forecasts for the assessment. Preserve every eligible opportunity and actual creation clock. No backdating, selecting a winner after viewing the same outcomes, or extending the window only because its interval is unfavorable.

Adaptive reuse can overfit the holdout itself,[14] and model-selection variance can be comparable to the differences between algorithms.[15] Past-only parameter fitting is necessary but does not correct research-level adaptation. Neither five thousand bootstrap draws nor rebranding 2024–25 as a replication makes the inherited specialist design unseen.

Conditional predictive-ability work distinguishes average forecast comparison from using current information to choose a future forecast.[16] This supports asking for an identified conditional mechanism before building a gate; it does not supply a small-sample theorem for nine LoL months. Monthly blocks retain the project’s agreed comparison convention, but they are not proof that dependence ends at month boundaries. Source-label sensitivity likewise does not authenticate independent editions.

Use one precommitted fixed-horizon assessment as the conservative default. If the eventual protocol allows repeated outcome looks or stopping when a threshold is crossed, a time-uniform confidence sequence is a possible separately specified alternative.[17] Such methods can handle adapted observations under appropriate conditional-mean and tail assumptions; they are **not universally IID-only**. They also do not automatically handle unknown payout bounds, arbitrary dependence, hidden selection or already inspected historical outcomes. No confidence-sequence implementation or new stopping rule was introduced here.

### The ±2pp target is demanding—and sample size cannot fix bias

For an illustrative independent-normal calculation, if the future error had zero mean and the current error variance remained stable,

\[
